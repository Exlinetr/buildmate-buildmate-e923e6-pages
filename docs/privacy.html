Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

exlinetr ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of developers using the BuildMate application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use BuildMate.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By installing or using BuildMate, you consent to the practices described in this document.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that enables BuildMate to deliver publishing workflows, marketing tools, and account synchronization for developers.

  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, and authentication identifiers created when signing up.
  • App Metadata & Content: App titles, descriptions, release notes, keywords, and uploaded store listing graphics created within the App.
  • Device & Usage Data: Operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, app performance metrics, and crash logs.

1.1 Developer Credentials

If you choose to integrate external developer accounts, credentials and tokens are stored securely with localized hardware encryption.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving BuildMate functions and marketing features.
  • Synchronizing your app publishing projects and assets across your authorized devices.
  • Responding to support requests, bug reports, and customer service inquiries.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing technical defects or unauthorized application usage.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or app publishing content to third parties.

  • Service Providers: Trusted vendor partners providing cloud infrastructure, database hosting, and customer support channels under strict data confidentiality terms.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation to protect legal rights and security.

Data Protection Commitment

Your app marketing concepts, metadata, and graphics remain your confidential intellectual property. We never scan or sell user project data to third parties.

4. Analytics and Third-Party Services

BuildMate uses aggregated, privacy-preserving analytics services to measure feature usage and performance metrics to help us optimize future releases.

  • Privacy-focused diagnostic and crash report collection.
  • Aggregated feature usage stats stripped of personal identifiers.

5. Data Retention and Security

We implement industry-standard physical, administrative, and technological security controls to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

  • Encryption of stored data at rest and in transit via TLS/SSL.
  • Account data retained only for as long as your account remains active or necessary to provide service.

Security Practices

API keys and store authentication tokens are handled inside secure system keychains on your device whenever supported.

6. Children's Privacy

BuildMate is designed for application developers and business professionals. It is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes and Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the 'Last Updated' date at the top of this page. Continued use of BuildMate after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
