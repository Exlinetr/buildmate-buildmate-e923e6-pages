Frequently Asked Questions

How does BuildMate assist with app publishing?
BuildMate simplifies your app release lifecycle by organizing metadata, store listing copy, release checklists, and asset guidelines in one central dashboard so you can deploy to app stores efficiently.
Can BuildMate automate my marketing asset creation?
Yes, BuildMate provides built-in tools to prepare and format promotional images, screenshot templates, and localized store listing descriptions tailored to App Store and Google Play guidelines.
Does BuildMate replace my Apple Developer or Google Play Console accounts?
No. BuildMate acts as a management layer and workspace assistant. You still maintain full control over your official developer accounts with Apple and Google.
Is my project data synced across devices?
BuildMate offers secure cloud synchronization when logged into your account, ensuring your store listings and marketing assets are accessible across all your supported devices.
Are my API keys and developer credentials secure?
All credentials and sensitive configuration keys entered into BuildMate are encrypted locally on your device and transmitted exclusively using end-to-end encrypted connections.
How do I manage or cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are managed directly through your App Store or Google Play Store account settings under active subscriptions.

Troubleshooting

App fails to sync metadata
Check your internet connection and verify that you are logged into your BuildMate account. If sync issues persist, try signing out and logging back in.
Exported screenshots appear blurry
Ensure your device display scaling is set to standard resolution and that high-density export is enabled in BuildMate asset settings.
Purchased features or credits not appearing
Go to the Settings page in BuildMate and tap 'Restore Purchases'. Make sure you are signed into the App Store / Play Store account used for purchase.
Validation errors on store listing text
Review the highlighted fields in the Store Guidelines Validator section for character overflow or forbidden promotional keywords.
App crashes during asset generation
Ensure your device has sufficient free storage space, close background applications, and verify you are running the latest version of BuildMate.
